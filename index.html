About Me

Who am i?

I'm Chiedozie Ikechukwu Nkulo, a Web Developer and visual UX/UI Designer

I’m a passionate Software Developer focused on building modern, practical, and intelligent digital solutions. I have experience across web development, AI, automation, and backend systems, with a strong interest in using technology to solve real-world problems. My development journey has given me hands-on experience working with technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, Tailwind CSS, React, Next.js, PHP, Python, Flask, MySQL, and PostgreSQL. I also have experience integrating APIs, working with databases, deploying applications, and building systems that combine software development with AI. One of my key projects is SentinelAI, an AI-powered malware detection system designed to analyze files and identify potentially malicious software. Building SentinelAI strengthened my experience in Python, machine learning, Flask, database management, and AI-driven application development. Beyond writing code, I’m interested in AI automation and emerging technologies, especially creating software that can automate repetitive processes, improve efficiency, and make complex tasks easier for people and businesses. I’m constantly learning, experimenting with new technologies, and turning ideas into working products. My goal is to build technology that is not only technically impressive, but also useful, scalable, and impactful.
